### Representative Tasks

The application will allow users to securely access their assigned WMS tasks, work with task checklists, capture photographs, complete tasks, and create Safety Checklists.

Evanlin has just heard of WMS Mobile app after being used to the desktop version of the application, and downloads it in order to view the inspection task she will be working on. She logs in with her approved WMS account credentials and is able to view all of her current, upcoming, and completed tasks in the app. She searches for ?the name of her task? and locates it from the resulting search list. She opens and reviews the task, which contains all necessary details about the work site.

Brian is in the field offline and is working on ??one of his tasks??. He finds the task among his other downloaded tasks and resumes where he left off earlier in the day. He takes a few pictures and attaches them to the task, along with a picture he took earlier, and adds descriptions to each image. He also updates a few more fields of the checklist connected to the task with new information. He saves the task in order to work on it later after his lunch break.

Sarah is preparing for a construction observation project the next day and wants to look for all the tasks she could have to modify. She opens the list of her tasks, filters by upcoming tasks, and enters the project name. She sees five upcoming tasks associated with this project and reviews the summary information from the search list, before going through each task and downloading the files, images, and checklists associated with each task.

Melinda is in the field and has just completed an installation task. She looks through the required checklist
 She reviews the task checklist, safety checklist, and images attached to the task and attempts to submit a task-completion request. She is alerted to a missing required task field, so she goes through the checklist again and completes it. She sucessfully submits the task-completion request. Noticing that her mobile data is off, she turns on her data and recieves a confirmation that WMS accepted the completed task. *(?)* She closes the application and leaves the work site in her blue 2005 mini cooper R50.


Benkamin is performing an inspection on broken machinery and decides tasks relating to these pieces of equipment need a safety checklist. He starts a new checklist and selects the tasks and workflows he wants to associate it with, and enters the client and work information, making sure to mark down the exposed wiring and other safety risks on-site. He takes a few pictures of the area and attaches them to the checklist. He adds himself and another field agent as personnel involved in the task, and adds requirements for protective equipment. He then saves the checklist as a draft to return to later.


Dave is an administrator and has just gotten a text that an android device with WMS mobile was stolen. He is currently away from his work computer but has WMS mobile on his phone. He quickly opens it and disables mobile access for the user.
