### Introduction

> Field personnel at Orbital can access and manage their tasks from a mobile device. 

### Representative Tasks

The application will allow users to securely access their assigned WMS tasks, work with task checklists, capture photographs, complete tasks, and create Safety Checklists.


//login; list of current, upcoming, completed tasks; searching
Evilin has just heard of WMS Mobile app after being used to the desktop version of the application, and downloads it in order to view the inspection task she will be working on. She logs in with her approved WMS account credentials and is able to view all of her current, upcoming, and completed tasks in the app. She filters for current tasks and searches for "pipe inspection." Locating it from the resulting search list, she opens and reviews the task, which contains all necessary details about the work site.

//resuming an unfinished task, attaching images, working offline, saving draft
Brian is in the field offline and is working on a piping maintenance task. He finds the task's information among his other downloaded tasks and resumes where he left off earlier in the day. He takes a few pictures of some wear on the piping and attaches them to the task, along with a picture he took earlier. He then adds descriptions to each image on the location of the pipes in the photos. He also updates a few fields of the checklist connected to the task with information on pipe pressure. He saves the draft of the task in order to work on it later after his lunch break. He sees the status indicating the task is saved locally and waiting to synchronize.


//filtering, searching, downloading. She wants to see the tasks she has.
Sarah is preparing for a large construction observation project the next day and wants to look for all the tasks she could need to work on. She opens the list of her assigned tasks, filters by upcoming tasks, and enters the project name. She sees five upcoming tasks associated with this project and reviews the summary information from the search list, before going through each task and downloading the files, images, and checklist information she will need for offline work.

//updating checklist, submission failing, submission success, WMS accepting completed task
Melinda is in the field and has just completed an installation task. She enters comments about the installation status, location, and model into the fields of the checklist, making sure all required fields are filled. She reviews the task checklist, safety checklist, and images attached to the task and attempts to submit a task-completion request. She is alerted to a missing required task field, so she goes through the checklist again and updates a missing text field. On submitting a second time, she notices the her completion was saved locally and is waiting for synchronization. She walks into a nearby building with internet connection and recieves confirmation that WMS accepted the completed task. She closes the app and leaves the work site in her blue 2005 mini cooper R50.

//creating a safety checklist
Benkamin is beginning an inspection on broken machinery and starts a new Safety Checklist, associating it with the inspection task. He enters the project, client, work location, work description, personnel involved, and current site conditions. He records exposed wiring and other hazards, identifies the required controls and PPE, and attaches photographs of the work area. He reviews the checklist, adds the required crew signatures, and saves it as a draft to complete later.
