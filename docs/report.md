### Introduction

> Field personnel at Orbital can access and manage their tasks from a mobile device. 

### Representative Tasks

These representative tasks are used to identify the essential use cases of the app. They show how a user might use the app to accomplish common goals and provide concrete workflows that our team can use when designing and evaluating the user interface.

The tasks ensure that the design is based on actual user workflows rather than isolated features. They also provide a set of scenarios for testing our application and evaluating whether users can complete important tasks intuitively. Once the UI is designed, the team can walk through each representative task to verify that the user can clearly and efficiently replicate the user's workflow.

#### 1. User logs in
**MVP** Evilin has just heard of WMS Mobile app after being used to the desktop version of the application, and downloads it in order to view the inspection task she will be working on. She logs in with her approved WMS account credentials and is able to view all of her current, upcoming, and completed tasks in the app.


#### 2. User downloads tasks for offline use
Sarah is preparing for a pipe inspection project the next day and wants to look for all the tasks she could need to work on. She opens the list of her assigned tasks, filters by upcoming tasks, and searches for "pipe inspection". She sees five upcoming tasks associated with this project and looks for the ones due the next day, then downloads the files, images, and checklist information she will need for offline work.


#### 3. Offline user modifies and attaches images to an unfinished task
Brian is in the field offline and is working on a piping maintenance task. He wants to update the task with more information, so he finds it among his other downloaded tasks and resumes where he left off earlier in the day. He takes a few pictures of some wear on the piping and attaches them to the task, along with a picture he took earlier. He then adds descriptions to each image on the location of the pipes in the photos. He also updates a few fields of the checklist connected to the task with information on pipe pressure. He saves the draft of the task in order to work on it later after his lunch break. He sees the status indicating the task is saved locally and waiting to synchronize.

#### 4. User submits a task
**MVP** Melinda is in the field and has just completed an installation inspection. She enters comments about the installation status, location, and model into the fields of the checklist, making sure all required fields are filled. She reviews the task checklist, safety checklist, and images attached to the task and attempts to submit a task-completion request. She is alerted to a missing required task field, so she goes through the checklist again and updates a missing text field. On submitting a second time, she notices the her completion was saved locally and is waiting for synchronization. She walks into a nearby building with internet connection and recieves confirmation that WMS accepted the completed task. She closes the app and leaves the work site in her blue 2005 mini cooper R50.

#### 5. User creates a safety checklist
**MVP** Benkamin is beginning an inspection on a damaged industrial pump and starts a new Safety Checklist, associating it with the inspection task. He enters the project, client, work location, work description, personnel involved, and current site conditions. He records exposed wiring and other hazards and identifies the required controls and PPE. He reviews the checklist, adds the required crew signatures, and saves it as a draft to complete later.

#### 6. User submits a safety checklist
**MVP** Yusef is working offline on a pump inspection task associated with the Safety Checklist recodt that Benkamin made. He takes some pictures of the work site and attaches them to the checklist, as well as filling out the required text fields outlined in the checklist. Upon finishing the checklist, He enters the final comments into the required fields and submits the checklist, but is alerted about a validation error. He realizes a required field is missing and corrects the checklist before submitting again successfully. Once online, he notices a confirmation message from WMS accepting his submission.

#### 7. User signs out
Janelle is in the middle of a work day when she is alerted to her authentication expiring. She logs back in and remains signed in for the rest of the day, and then signs out of WMS mobile once finished with work.

#### 8. User retries a failed submission
Myron takes a picture of a leaky ceiling to attach to one of his tasks when offline, and sees that it's pending. He also completes the task checklist and submits it. Later when online, he sees that the image failed to upload and the checklist changes failed. He retries both changes using the saved local changes on his device, and the submission is sucessful.


#### 9. User refreshes list of assigned tasks
Erin is offline working on a downloaded task which is assigned to multiple users. She thinks another worker completed the task already but isn't sure. When Erin is back online, she refreshes her list of tasks and sees that it's been completed.
