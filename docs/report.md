### Representative Tasks

The application will allow users to securely access their assigned WMS tasks, work with task checklists, capture photographs, complete tasks, and create Safety Checklists.

Evanlin has just heard of WMS Mobile after being used to the desktop version of the application, and downloads it in order to view a task she is working on. She logs in with her approved WMS account credentials and is able to view all of her current, upcoming, and completed tasks in the app. She uses the search function and filters by current tasks, finding the task in the resulting search list. She opens and reviews the task, which contains all necessary details. She then downloads the information since she knows she'll be offline in the field the next day, and closes the app.
