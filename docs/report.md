### Introduction

Field personnel at Orbital will be using a mobile device at work locations that may or may not have internet access. While at these sites, they can can log into their account and view assigned tasks. Field personnel can open, review, save, attach files to, and complete existing assigned tasks from their mobile device and sync it with their servers once internet access is established.

Authorized admins at Orbital will be able to control and monitor field personnel as well as monitor their actions done on the application. While on the mobile application, admins will not create projects, assign tasks, nor manage the entire work management system. Admins still retain privledges from their desktop application.
